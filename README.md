# kiddie-game

**Nina's Toverbakkerij** — a magic-bakery game in Dutch for a 4-year-old, built
in SwiftUI for iPad.

**Where it is:** five rooms are built. [app/](app/) holds **De Bakkerij**, the
hub and the wall of twelve frames; **De Tuin**, where she sows, waters and picks
five magic ingredients; **De Keuken** — one full round (roll, fetch, stir, pour,
bake) with seven toys; **Versieren**, where the cake she just baked goes onto a
turntable and she covers it in sprinkles, candles, hearts, stars, crowns, fruit
and cream; and **Het Feest**, a disco with a mirror ball, a DJ, a light-up dance
floor and six teddy-bear friends who each dance a different little move to
whatever beat she taps. Start at [app/README.md](app/README.md).

**Next:** test it with Nina. The one asset still missing is the party's backing
loop. Versieren's required spine from `GAMEPLAY.md` §6.4 is the remaining
design-to-code gap.
